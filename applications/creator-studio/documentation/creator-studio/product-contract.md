# Creator Studio working product contract

**Direction authorized:** Aaron Alderman, 9 November 2026. This is the product
contract for implementation. Achieved capability is recorded separately in the
[implementation ledger](implementation-and-assurance.md).

## One project through seven apps

A professional must be able to maintain a body of work without manually
coordinating copied passages, cell addresses, slide numbers and filenames.
Stable identities and explicit versioned relationships are maintained by the
system and remain inspectable. The promise is managed references, not the
absence of references or the disappearance of genuine ambiguity.

| App | Core work | Shared subjects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documents | Import supported material, retain its original, draft and restructure sections, bind quantities and claims, standardize, compare and compile. | Sections, claims, definitions, sources, requirements and decisions. |
| Tables and Models | Define named inputs and units, calculate derived quantities, inspect dependencies, compare scenarios and compile tables and charts. | Quantities, expressions, assumptions, units and observations. |
| Presentations | Compose audience-specific arguments, edit slides and speaker notes, select maintained figures, review and export. | Findings, sections, quantities, charts and narrative arrangements. |
| Canvas | Map entities and directed relationships, edit labels and arrangement, inspect connections and compile diagrams. | Identified entities, relation kinds, explanations and presentation coordinates. |
| Media | Compose scripts, scenes, timing, cues and captions, retain asset references and attribution, preview supported representations and compile production material. | Scripts, beats, assets, timing, captions and sources. |
| Workflows | Choose or compose an approach, bind inputs, validate steps, preview consequences, execute allowed stages, retain results and repeat. | Processes, parameters, dependencies, checkpoints and responsibilities. |
| Review and Commentary | Comment on identified subjects and versions, propose revisions, compare alternatives, accept or refuse changes, retain unresolved work and export review packets. | Attributed questions, suggestions, decisions, exact bases and histories. |

Every app exposes relevant commentary, standards and inspection. Review also
provides a project-wide view. A surface change, semantic change, standards
change, workflow change and comment are distinct operations with distinct
consequences.

## Shared semantic model

The project retains identified subjects with declared types and fields,
relationships, source material, selected standards, compositions and accepted
versions. Presentation choices can vary without silently changing shared
meaning. Two contributors may use different terms or definitions: a proposed
mapping must expose the agreement, information loss and remaining difference.

References select identified subjects and properties. Renaming a visible label
must not rename an identity or sever commentary. A source map explains which
subject and field produced each managed output. Unsupported imported material
is retained with its original and an explicit interpretation gap.

Compilation derives an artifact from an exact accepted project and profile.
Decompilation recovers only what a declared grammar can account for. An inverse
edit is offered only where correspondence is supported and checked. Layout,
semantic values, uncertain interpretation and approval must not be conflated.

## Standards

Standards are reusable, versioned definitions: terminology, units, styles,
templates, accessibility rules, attribution and output requirements. A project
selects its standards explicitly. Composing incompatible standards produces a
reviewable conflict. Changes to a maintained standard produce an impact preview;
they do not silently upgrade existing projects or authorize publication.

## Commentary and decisions

A comment records its author attribution, subject, field or representation,
exact revision, original context and status. Attributions supplied by an import
or local persona are distinguished from verified identity. A comment can be
visible in several views while retaining its original context. An ambiguous or
obsolete anchor remains visible as unresolved rather than moving to convenient
new text. Resolving a discussion does not itself accept a semantic edit.

Suggested revisions identify an exact basis, target, proposed value and reason.
Preview shows affected subjects, calculations and representations. Acceptance
rechecks the basis, validates the complete managed state and retains the exact
change and its receipt. Stale bases, undeclared fields, invalid references and
unsupported effects refuse without changing accepted state.

## Concrete professional composition

The first whole-suite composition is a client training proposal. Its sample
figures are illustrative: 30 participants, six sessions, AUD 40 materials per
participant and AUD 500 facilitation per session. The base calculated total is
AUD 4,200. At 48 participants it is AUD 4,920 if all other assumptions remain
unchanged. This arithmetic does not establish that the staffing or programme
design remains appropriate at the larger size.

1. Retain the client brief and identify its assumptions.
2. Choose a budget-first, narrative-first or co-design journey; the apps can
   also be opened directly and used in parallel.
3. Maintain the proposal in Documents, quantities in Tables, delivery map in
   Canvas, client briefing in Presentations and script/captions in Media.
4. Attach the client's request for 48 places to the participant assumption.
5. Preview a scenario, affected calculations, passages, slides and media
   material while preserving the 30-person baseline.
6. Record unresolved facilitation questions, review the exact proposed change,
   and accept or refuse it.
7. Compile deliverables from one identified accepted version. Repeat the
   composition with new inputs for a subsequent cohort.

Changing a script line that represents an interpretation must not rewrite the
underlying data. Changing an input can recalculate declared dependencies but
cannot silently resolve a professional judgment or publish the result.

## Compositions, improvement and exchange

A composition defines available steps, allowed operations, input/output
contracts, optional paths and checkpoints. A journey is the particular route
taken. Learning introduces common inspection and correction operations through
use; essential access must remain available directly rather than hidden behind
a puzzle or mandatory lesson.

People can exchange versioned compositions, standards, models, assets and review
services. A package declares its author attribution, licence, required inputs,
effects, exact versions, checks and known limits. An imported package remains a
proposal until the receiving project inspects and accepts its use. Commercial
terms and local records are distinguished from actual payment settlement and
protected delivery. Buying a method does not establish that its claims are true.

Improvement records a specific change and evidence against named projects or
cases. Multiple presentations of one run are one evidence family. Users choose
whether and when to adopt successors; uncertainty and faithful disagreement are
legitimate outcomes.

## Whole-app acceptance

Each app must create or edit real managed work and produce a usable output;
seven navigation cards are insufficient. Whole-suite tests must demonstrate
one shared change, isolated presentation changes, shared commentary, selected
standards, multiple journeys, scenario preservation, refusal without mutation,
exact replay, export/import and composition exchange. The product must present
actual capability rather than turning an unavailable operation into a button
that merely reports success.
