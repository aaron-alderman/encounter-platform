# Blinded test of slopes, ratchets, and paths back on Claimipedia decision lineages

## Scope and sample

Use five purposively selected real operational decision lineages retained in the Claimipedia development corpus: data-authority adoption, dual-base serving activation, parallel-lane migration capability activation, bounded primary routing, and predecessor custody. These were selected after a source-availability and ordering scout. Their exact documents and return targets are frozen in the runner and manifest. None of the selected documents was an input in the prior IAC7/SRP7 authority-resolver and reset comparisons. The lineages are fresh evaluation targets, not unseen to the investigator, and several share an operational programme. They are five scoped decisions rather than five independent organizational settings.

For each lineage freeze three ordered records with an earlier prefix of two and a later prefix of three. Order follows documentary dependencies when timestamps share a date. The return target stays identical across the two prefixes. Preserve each full record in the packet and label it only by ordinal position. No edits to the source documents or substitutions after freezing. Hash the protocol, rubric, code, source documents and prior scope records before structured annotation. This is a read-only study of recorded decisions, not a request to execute any return.

## Annotation and blinding

Code the five fields using the separate review rubric. The first three are observed versus projected continuation, realized versus pending added reversal burden, and maturity of the path back. Separately identify who can choose and the incremental work or resource they would bear. The investigator records evidence spans and reasons for both cuts, with the earlier annotation frozen before the later annotation. The investigator already scouted portions of the documents for eligibility and is not blinded.

Use two fresh separate reviewers, each with one shuffled opaque-ID packet, the same rubric and no prior conversation, investigator marks, peer judgments, other cut, or lineages list. They judge each case using only the documents available in their packet. A source quote must match the supplied text, and an observed slope needs evidence from two distinct records. Reviewers may infer temporal progress from the documents. Different readers judge the two cuts, so changes in their labels cannot by themselves identify the effect of receiving a later document.

## Outcomes

Report exact investigator–reviewer agreement by field across ten cuts, alongside agreement where both assign a known status. Report known class coverage so shared unknowns cannot substitute for agreement about mechanisms. A field has descriptive support for the distinction if at least two different known statuses appear in both readers' assignments and agreement among jointly known cuts is at least 70%. For each lineage display the early-to-late statuses for the slope, ratchet and path. Count exact transition agreement and how often both readers detected a change. For chooser and cost report the roles and obligations in the source evidence and any missing estimates. Do not infer present authority, live system state or real cost estimates from archival documents. No significance tests or out-of-sample accuracy claims are planned.
