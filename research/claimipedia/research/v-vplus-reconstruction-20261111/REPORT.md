# V and V+ reconstruction results

The codec, scoring rules and eight held-out record IDs were frozen before opening the selected texts. The essential meaning probes were written and hashed before encoding. The held-out claims came from the claimipedia and corpus400 namespaces, with four per namespace. Their IDs and known exact-text or reviewed equivalents were outside the previous crystallization sample and the four design examples. Namespace is a record category. The test used one investigator to write probes, encode and judge decoded content, with arm structure visible.

| V + V+ + coupling | 1 / 8 | 5 / 24 | 1 |
| V + V+ with coupling removed | 0 / 8 | 4 / 24 | 1 |

Adding coupling gained one probe on one record, with no probe losses. The preregistered coupling criterion required gains on at least two records and an increase in exact reconstructions. The full arm also fell short of the preregistered majority-exact threshold. “Exact” refers to the frozen essential meaning probes and the absence of consequential unsupported assertions, rather than identical wording.

The successful record was decoded as an agent exercising choice, the other retaining choice and being able to act, and the former requiring the other to retain choice. The uncoupled arm supplied the two coordinates alone. The investigator treated choice plus capacity to act as the operational expression of agency for this record.

## Verification and checkpoint reflection

The scorer checked the frozen corpus, split, codec, protocol and probe hashes, replayed the decoder and checked every arm against the stored output. An independent tally of the judgment flags reproduced the counts. A direction reversal changed the decoded coupling while preserving the uncoupled output, and invalid endpoints and unknown coupling kinds were rejected. One draft encoding used appeal as a verb and the validator rejected it before decoding. It was expressed using the frozen vocabulary as access to appeal. No vocabulary, selection, probe or scoring rule changed.

The result bounds a claim about this specific representation. One held-out claim retained a requirement only through the directed link. The strong reconstruction claim failed. The narrow vocabulary and atomic role slots leave modality scope, disjunction, qualifiers and several domain predicates underexpressed. The AI responsibility claim shows that filling a role slot can narrow the source as well as omit it. This run cannot separate the limits of the two-coordinate thesis from the limits of the frozen codec or the investigator's coding decisions. The disposition is to retain the codec as a research prototype and withhold any corpus-wide adequacy or migration claim. The checkpoint adds no scientific admission or application integration.
