# Blinded judgment of how power is used

Read each record independently. Judge the relationship the text portrays or explicitly proposes. Your task is to classify the portrayal, including an explicitly hypothetical or normative arrangement. You are not being asked whether the source is true, whether the actor is good in general, or how strongly you agree.

- **service**. The text presents a use or restraint of influence as substantively answering to the legitimate needs, purposes, welfare, or standing of those affected. A good intention alone is insufficient. Look for what the arrangement is said to do or be for.
- **control**. The text presents an exercise of influence as instrumentalizing people, securing alignment to the authority's purposes, or subordinating those affected to an external objective. A use of authority, restriction, or coordination alone is insufficient.
- **mixed**. The text presents substantive service and control together, or gives them as comparably central contrasting possibilities without a single focal portrayal.
- **unclear**. There is no identifiable exercise or restraint of power, no clear orientation, or too little information to distinguish the two. Preserve conditional and speculative scope in your explanation.

A source can describe a protection that constrains someone, and a source can describe nominally helpful conduct that is controlling. Ground your decision in the stated relationship and purpose. Use only the supplied text. Return one JSON entry per opaque ID with a lower-case `label` from the four above and a concise `reason`. Do not seek other study material.
