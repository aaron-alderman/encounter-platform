# Advisory versus mandatory lexical cues for Claimipedia causal links

## Question

Does making lexical cues advisory recover supported meaning lost by the hard whitelist while continuing to reject unsupported proposals? Preserve the earlier representation of an event-to-event edge and a distinct strength relation whose modifier applies to an existing cause–outcome link. This checks fidelity to recorded claims, including evidential or intake interpretations. It does not validate the causal claims themselves.

## Frozen records

Use 11 records fresh relative to the previous experiments. Two purposive real effect-strength positives were inspected during retrieval before preregistration. Use the unedited finding on network-position moderation from the Claimipedia third-order influence evidence corpus and the unedited RIO-041 interpretation on resonance amplifying a mechanical response from the Claimipedia research inbox. Neither is a synthetic test string. The second is an intake interpretation, not an independently verified finding.

Select six chain-cue and three control-cue claims from both parts of the identity map, in that order, with the same cue expressions as the prior study. Control records lack both chain and effect-selection cues. Rank by SHA-256 of `claimipedia-advisory-v1|` plus record ID. Exclude the four original discussion examples, the crystallization, reconstruction and causal-check samples, and their transitive confirmed-equivalence and normalized exact-text matches. Keep at most one per component. Freeze the complete records' source files, selected IDs, protocol and implementation hashes before opening the nine hash-selected texts. Keep every selected record. Do not infer natural strength coverage from cue membership. Report the two purposive records separately.

## Inputs and conditions

Freeze reference links and exact role spans before writing ordinary proposals. Encode one first-pass ordinary proposal set per claim. Freeze proposals, then prepare and freeze typed source witnesses from the claim texts. The same investigator prepares all three artifacts. Keep that limitation explicit. The checker gets the source and its digest, node spans, proposals, and witnesses. The scorer alone uses the reference link set. Source snippets and role anchors use exact Python character offsets. A witness must match type, ordered endpoints or modifier, signed direction, modality and condition, and cover the referenced spans. A source-verified snippet still depends on human interpretation.

Run the same proposals and witnesses in each condition:

- Unchecked keeps all links.
- Hard uses the original frozen whitelist checker, requiring both an exact typed witness and a registered lexical cue.
- Advisory requires the same typed witness and preserves the cue result in its decision receipt, even when the cue is absent.

Both checks only remove proposed links. Their matching differences must agree with cue enforcement on the same inputs. Score unique supported and unsupported links, omitted reference links and records with at least one unsupported link. Meaning lost is the count of reference-supported links present unchecked and absent in that arm. Report ordinary and effect-strength results separately, including the number of genuine strength links retained. The desired ordinary result is less meaning lost than hard, fewer unsupported links than unchecked, and zero checker-induced supported loss. If the unchecked ordinary arm contains no unsupported proposals, state that the reduction is unmeasured there.

## Challenge and annotation-error control

Use the earlier deterministic challenge generator on frozen reference links. Pair a supported chain with an invented strength modifier on the same roles, or a supported strength link with an invented modifier-to-cause edge. Give zero-link records a single invented edge when two nodes are available. Score these separately from ordinary proposals. Then repeat only the type-confusion cases with an additional deliberately incorrect witness matching the decoy, anchored to the entire real record. This estimates sensitivity to these injected annotation errors, not their natural prevalence. Keep references and proposals unchanged. Report whether the lexical veto sometimes helps against a bad witness, along with its supported-link costs. No statistical inference or automatic extraction claim is planned.
