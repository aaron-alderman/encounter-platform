# Scoped V/V+ reconstruction on fresh Claimipedia records

## Question and sample

Test whether a richer closed-vocabulary codec can express the essential meaning of fresh identity claims and whether adding directed cross-coordinate rules improves reconstruction. Keep V as the subject's own capacities, choices, costs or judgments and V+ as the arrangements involving other parties, institutions or relations. Coordinates contain scoped formulas with typed participant references. A rule composes formula references from both coordinates. The same investigator writes probes, encodes and evaluates controlled-language decodes with arm visible. This is an expressibility and ablation study with manual encoding.

Select four identity-map records each from the aiInquiry and claimipedia namespaces, eligible if tagged agency, attribution or relation. Exclude the crystallization sample, the first reconstruction holdout and four earlier discussion examples. Block human-reviewed equivalents and case-folded whitespace-normalized exact text, transitively, and select at most one record per resulting component. In namespace order aiInquiry then claimipedia, rank by ascending SHA-256 of the literal `claimipedia-scoped-v2|` followed by record ID. Freeze corpus and earlier split hashes, protocol, selector, codec and selected IDs before displaying the eight texts. Retain all eight and make no outcome-driven changes to the frozen vocabulary, sampling rules or codec. Namespaces are record categories.

## Representation and arms

Controlled predicates have fixed arities. Roles are shared across coordinates, and scoped terms express, for example, the decision of a specified participant. Formula operators preserve negation, modality, conjunction, disjunction, directional relations, and quantified or contextual scope. Explicitly distinguish asserted clauses from fragments that are only used within a rule. Cross-coordinate links contain references and logical operators, with no new atomic propositions. No field can pass through source prose.

Use one manual encoding per record. The coupled arm sees roles, coordinates, clause status and rendered cross-coordinate rules. The uncoupled arm sees identical roles, coordinates and status with just the rules omitted. The result measures the information carried by the authored link in this representation. The experiment does not establish that a different arrangement of local formulas could not carry the same information.

## Probes and decision rules

After displaying the frozen texts and before encoding, author two to eight essential meaning probes per claim. Tag each probe as local or coupling, with a coupling probe requiring a specific rule across a subject-level and a relational or institutional statement. Freeze probes by SHA-256 before encoding. Score each probe as preserved only when the output conveys its essential roles, modality, direction and qualifiers. Explicit fragment content counts for a local probe that asks only for content in that scope. It cannot count as an asserted conclusion or a cross-fragment rule. Probe labels are analytic annotations. A legitimate standalone clause may satisfy a coupling-tagged probe if it explicitly preserves the needed meaning. Adjacent statements alone do not entail a rule. Flag any consequential unsupported assertion, scope error or reversal. Exact reconstruction requires all essential probes and no such assertions.

Report exact counts, overall and local/coupling probe recall, unsupported-assertion records and paired gains and losses for every record. Retain the previous descriptive criteria. The coupling contribution criterion requires gains on at least two records and more exact reconstructions than the uncoupled arm. The full arm meets the adequacy threshold if it reconstructs a majority of records exactly. No inference beyond the sampled records is planned. Compare the arms on this fresh holdout and treat any comparison with the earlier holdout as descriptive only. Report encoding or scoring limitations and deviations.
